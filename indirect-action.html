Indirect Action

Support for public interest missions.

Collective action problems tend to stand in the way of systems change. Many people want to collectively organize in the face of big challenges like online social networking market capture, but are unsure where to begin.

Where we are invited and able, we work with individuals and organizations to help create the conditions for those interested to participate in shaping systems change through systemic coinquiry and indirect action.

We currently provide facilitation support to Ecosystem Action Research (EAR), to help make public interest internet technology legible, usable, and accessible to everyday people. EAR is a project of the Raft Foundation.
