Strategy & Operations Alliance

A forthcoming community of practice for partnership-oriented strategy and operations professionals.

It’s an uncertain time for businesses, organizations, and markets. With the rapid advance of technological capabilities, expansion of market capture, and shifts in how people think about labor, synergy between strategy and operations have never been so critical to success.

Current norms in business can include siloed decision-making focused on near-term targets, while missing opportunities for employee autonomy and loyalty, sustainable business modeling, and a shared understanding of what long-term success looks like.

We see an opportunity to rethink and regenerate how organizations approach strategy and operations to meet the moment and prepare for the future.

The Strategy and Operations Alliance is a planned community of practice and membership organization that exists to provide participation pathways and member services to enable S&O professionals to work together to imagine and realize desirable futures for how management works.
