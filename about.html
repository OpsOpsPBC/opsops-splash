About Ops Ops PBC

Ops Ops PBC is a research and innovation lab that exists to bring about a partnership-oriented approach to strategy and operations.

Ops Ops supports four initiatives.

  • Org Dev Lab, a consultancy providing organizational development services to teams navigating complex adaptive environments
  • Strategy & Operations Alliance, an emerging community of practice for partnership-oriented S&O professionals
  • Strategy Theory, a community-maintained resource exploring the potential of cybersystemic practice
  • Indirect Action, facilitation support for public interest missions
